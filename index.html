OUR STORY

Authentic Ethiopian Coffee

Ethiopia is the birthplace of coffee and home to some of the world's most distinctive coffee origins. DAG Ethiopian Coffee connects Ethiopian coffee producers with coffee lovers, importers and roasters around the world. Our mission is to share the quality, heritage and unique character of Ethiopian coffee with the world.
