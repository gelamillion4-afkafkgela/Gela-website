OUR STORY

Authentic Ethiopian Coffee.
Shared with the world.

Ethiopia is the birthplace of coffee and home to some of the world's most distinctive coffee origins. DAG Ethiopian Coffee aims to connect Ethiopian coffee producers with coffee lovers, importers and roasters.

Our focus is on sharing the quality, heritage and unique character of Ethiopian coffee while building reliable relationships with buyers.
